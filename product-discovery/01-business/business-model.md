# Business Model

This document explains how the **Postific** product creates value for customers and how the business earns revenue sustainably.

The Business Model is the basis for decisions on product strategy, monetization, and business growth.

---

# Overview

The product uses a **Software as a Service (SaaS)** model with a subscription system.

Customers get access to the platform through subscription plans that fit their organization's needs.

This model enables sustainable product development while delivering long-term value to customers.

---

# Value Creation

The product creates value by helping organizations:

- Reduce the time needed to manage social media.
- Simplify the marketing workflow.
- Improve collaboration between team members.
- Support data-based decision making.
- Reduce the complexity of using many tools.

The product's main value is not just providing features, but improving the team's operational efficiency.

---

# Customer Segments

The product is aimed at:

## Primary Segment

- Marketing Team

## Secondary Segments

- Startup
- Digital Agency

All product development will prioritize the needs of these segments.

---

# Revenue Model

For the MVP phase, the product follows a **Free Access** strategy to validate Product-Market Fit.

The main hypothesis for the medium-term revenue model is:

- Monthly subscription.
- Annual subscription.

The final monetization model will be set after real usage is validated and must align with `pricing-strategy.md`.

Additional revenue may be considered at a later development stage if it fits the product vision.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

The pricing strategy is based on these principles:

- Easy to understand.
- Transparent.
- Grows with the customer.
- Delivers value proportional to the benefit received.

Pricing plan details are covered in the `pricing-strategy.md` document.

---

# Customer Value Proposition

Customers choose this product because they get:

- A simpler workflow.
- A modern user experience.
- Better team collaboration.
- An integrated platform.
- Higher operational efficiency.

---

# Cost Considerations

The main cost components include:

- Cloud infrastructure.
- Third-party service providers (for example, social media integration).
- Product development.
- Operations and customer support.

This document does not cover operational costs in financial detail.

---

# Growth Strategy

Product growth is planned through:

- Acquiring new customers.
- Retaining customers.
- Increasing customer value through additional features.
- Developing the product based on user needs.
- Improving the quality of the user experience.

---

# Business Principles

All business decisions follow these principles:

- Customer Value First.
- Sustainable Growth.
- Simplicity.
- Long-Term Product Thinking.
- Data-Informed Decision Making.

---

# Assumptions

The Business Model is currently built on these assumptions:

- Organizations are willing to pay to improve team work efficiency.
- A simpler workflow delivers real business value.
- Collaboration is an important need for the target market.
- AI will be part of the modern way of working, not just an add-on feature.
- A free access strategy in the early phase will speed up validation of market needs before monetization.

These assumptions will continue to be validated as the product develops.

---

# Success Criteria

The Business Model is considered successful when:

- The value delivered to customers has been clearly defined.
- The customer segments have been determined.
- The main revenue sources have been identified.
- The business growth principles are documented.
- The business model supports the product's long-term vision.
