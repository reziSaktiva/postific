# S-006 · Per-post Engage following the Buffer pattern

**Docs:** ADR-157 (Accepted), ADR-040, ADR-046, ADR-097, ADR-140, ADR-149, ADR-153, `product-discovery/04-ux/key-screen-patterns.md` (KSP-06, KSP-D06), `apps/web/.claude/CLAUDE.md`
**Tickets:** T-216 through T-222 and T-224 (`tickets/v04-engagement-mvp.md`); T-223 Deferred

Pattern source: REA observation of `publish.buffer.com/community` (2026-11-05). The pattern is imitated (layout and behavior), not the code, assets, or text. Data and adapter capabilities do not grow except for the new status.

## Behavior

Seen from the side of the user (Raka) who opens Engage.

**Layout**

- A By post / List toggle in the header (Q5, King Rezi 2026-11-05). By post is the default view; List shows comments as a list, its content determined by the T-216.5 design.
- Engage (By post view) shows two panels inside the content: the post list on the left, the comments of the selected post on the right. The list panel can be collapsed.
- A post card contains the platform icon, account name, caption snippet, thumbnail if any, a badge with the count of unreplied comments, and a thin progress bar (the ratio of comments already handled).
- Posts without comments are hidden by default; one control shows all of them.
- Comments without a `postId` are shown in a group of their own.
- The right panel contains a post preview card (platform, account, date, media, truncated caption), a summary "x dibalas / y total komentar", then the comment list.
- A dismissible information banner may be shown above the right panel (e.g. old comments); its content and exact text are determined by the T-216 design.

**Filter and sort**

- Main filters in the header: Account/Channels (dropdown) and Status (a single-select dropdown with checkmarks: Semua, Belum dibalas, Dibalas, Selesai, Diabaikan). The label text is determined by the T-216 design.
- One icon button opens a panel containing Sort By (unreplied first as the default, newest, oldest), Date Range (7 days, 30 days, 6 months, 12 months), and Reset filters.
- The Platform filter remains (a capability today) and lives in the Filter & Sort panel (King Rezi's decision 2026-11-05 on the T-216 design).

**Comment status**

- Statuses: `unread`, `replied`, `resolved`, `dismissed` (ADR-157).
- Per-comment actions appear on hover: Resolve and Dismiss; Reply opens the reply area. Reversible: a comment that is already Resolved or Dismissed can be reopened (`unread`), per the answer to Q3 of ADR-157 (yes, reversible).
- Comments in the "Tanpa posting" group cannot be replied to from Postific (Outstand only replies per post, ADR-113): the composer is disabled with a clear message; Resolve and Dismiss remain available.
- A reply that is sent successfully marks the comment `replied` if it was `unread` (Q2). It does not downgrade `resolved` or `dismissed`.
- The sidebar badge and the Engagement Snapshot on Home still count `unread` only (Q4), limited to the last 12 months, the same as the default Date Range of the post list (King Rezi's decision 2026-11-06).
- Data is still updated through periodic pull and manual refresh; no Supabase Realtime (ADR-023). The sync zone (KSP-06-F07, F08) is kept.

**State**

- Empty, filter with no results, refresh in progress, and refresh failed still follow KSP-06 State Handling; the text is aligned by the T-216 design.
- There is no tab or state for Mentions, Direct Message, or webhooks (ADR-040).

## Test seams

- Domain `engagement`: status transitions and the `replied` rule in `engagement.service` (unit tests, local double in the test file, not the production singleton, `AGENTS.md` rule 11).
- Repository `lib/repositories/engagement`: per-post query, unreplied count, sort, and date range (DB integration tests where available, like the T-150 pattern).
- Server Action in `app/(app)/engage/actions.ts`: only calls the service (hard rule no. 3).
- UI: Playwright E2E golden path for Engage on `local` (ADR-149); light, dark, and 375px variants observed in the browser.
- Verified (staging) once per wave (ADR-153).

## Out of scope

- The Mentions tab and all Instagram DM or story mention paths (T-223, Deferred).
- Inbox assignment (T-055, Could Have).
- Realtime, the `comment.received` webhook, and nested threads (ADR-040, ADR-023).
- Changes to the Outstand adapter.
