# S-008 · Publish redesign following the Buffer Publish pattern

**Docs:** ADR-165 (Accepted), ADR-097, ADR-140, ADR-149, ADR-153, ADR-158, ADR-161, `product-discovery/02-product/roles-permissions.md` (content statuses, rule 4), `product-discovery/04-ux/key-screen-patterns.md` (KSP-02, KSP-03, KSP-04), `product-discovery/04-ux/information-architecture.md` (Publish), `apps/web/.claude/CLAUDE.md`
**Design:** `ScreenPublishRedesain` in the Postific artifact, confirmed by King Rezi 2026-11-24 (gallery entry "Publish: redesain pola Buffer", 28 scenarios)
**Tickets:** T-286 to T-295 (`tickets/v02-publishing-mvp.md`)

Pattern source: REA observation of `publish.buffer.com/schedule` (2026-11-24, desktop, 768 px and 390 px; Calendar, List, Board, post preview, expanded modal, video). The pattern is imitated (layout and behavior), not the code, assets, branding or text. Outstand gains no new capability; the best-time suggestion uses data Postific already stores.

## Behavior

Seen from the side of the user (Raka) who opens Publish.

**Page and views**

- Publish is one page. The header holds the Publish title, a view switcher **Calendar · List · Board**, and "+ New Post". The route tabbar Calendar / Queue / Drafts / History is gone.
- The page reopens the view last used by that user (Calendar when there is none). There is no "Save current view" control.
- The old addresses `/publish/calendar`, `/publish/queue`, `/publish/drafts`, `/publish/history` and `/publish/history/[postId]` still work and land on the matching view, tab or post. Deep links from notifications, Home and the MCP surface keep working.
- A timezone picker on the toolbar changes how times are shown on Publish only. It starts at the Profile timezone (T-147) and never writes the Profile.

**Calendar**

- Toolbar: previous and next, the current range label, Today, Month or Week; on the right Filter (channels), "No date" (opens a side panel listing posts without a date), an options menu, and the timezone picker.
- Month: each day shows up to 3 post chips (platform icon, time, status icon, thumbnail with a ▶ mark for video), then "+N lainnya", which opens a popover listing that day's posts. A future day shows a schedule button on hover that opens the Draft Editor with that date set.
- Week: hourly rows with a current-time line. The week starts on Monday, as today.
- One chip per post target (account), as today. Statuses shown are the ones that have a date (Scheduled, Published, Failed), as KSP-02-F02.

**Post preview**

- Clicking a chip opens a popover next to it: date and time, content format, Expand; account and status; caption; media; for published posts the metrics with a link to insights; the footer action depends on status (Go to post, Edit, Retry) plus a more-actions menu.
- Expand opens a dialog with the same content, the result of every account of the same post (the History Detail pattern), and "Published via <network>". No "Add note".
- A published **video plays inline** in the popover and the dialog with the browser's native video player (play/pause, time, progress, volume, fullscreen). When Postific has no playable video URL for that post (for example an imported post that only has a stored thumbnail, ADR-158), the thumbnail is shown with "Go to post" and no play control.

**List**

- Line tabs with counts: **Queue · Drafts · Approvals · History**. Posts are grouped by day with the time on the left (above the card on tablet).
- Queue: scheduled posts ordered by publish time, as KSP-03 and ADR-083.
- Drafts: `Draft` and `Ready to Schedule` content.
- Approvals: `In Review` content with the existing actions Mark Ready (`In Review` → `Ready to Schedule`) and Back to Draft (`In Review` → `Draft`). All roles that may do these transitions today may do them here (`roles-permissions.md`); nothing is blocked waiting for approval and `In Review` can still be skipped.
- History: published and failed posts as cards with metrics, "Published via", Go to post; failed posts show the error and Retry.

**Empty Queue: best-time suggestions**

- When the Queue has no scheduled posts, it shows suggested time slots per connected account, each row with the platform icon and "+ New" that opens the Draft Editor with that account and time set, plus a control to show more times.
- Suggestions come from that account's own published posts in the last 90 days: each post's publish time, in the timezone shown on the page, is put in a weekday-and-hour bucket; buckets are ranked by the median engagement rate of their posts from `AnalyticsPostMetric`, ties broken by median reach. A bucket needs at least 3 posts to count. Suggestions are the next upcoming occurrences of the top buckets within the next 7 days, 3 per account by default.
- An account with fewer than 10 published posts with metrics in the window gets no suggestion; when no account has suggestions, the Queue shows a normal empty state with "+ New Post".
- Suggestions are read-only hints; they create nothing until the user schedules a post.
- The thresholds above (90 days, median engagement rate then median reach, at least 3 posts per bucket, at least 10 posts per account, 3 suggestions per account over the next 7 days) were confirmed by King Rezi on 2026-11-24.

**Board**

- Columns **Draft · Approvals · Scheduled · Published · Failed** with counts and horizontal scroll. `Ready to Schedule` cards sit in Draft with a "Ready to Schedule" badge. Grouping is by Status only.
- Cards show the date or "No date", status badge, account with platform badge, caption, thumbnail, and an actions menu (on hover on desktop, always on phone). Clicking a card opens the same post preview.

**Tablet and phone**

- Tablet (768 px): the app sidebar becomes an icon rail; month chips become platform icons only with a "+N" chip; Board shows about two columns.
- Phone (390 px): a top bar with the menu, "+ New Post" becomes a "+" icon button, the view switcher shows icons only; Calendar switches to a 3-day view with a date picker (shadcn Calendar); the List tabs become a dropdown; Board shows one column per screen.
- Light and dark follow the existing tokens; no new tokens.

## Test seams

- Domain `analytics`: a best-time use case that takes per-post publish times and metrics and returns ranked weekday-hour buckets and the next slot times (unit tests with a local double, not the production singleton, `AGENTS.md` rule 11). Exposed through the `analytics` public API; Publish calls it through an Application Service (hard rules 3 and 5).
- Repository `lib/repositories/analytics`: the query that returns publish time and metrics per account for the window (DB integration test where available).
- Domain `publishing`: grouping for the Board columns (including `Ready to Schedule` in Draft) and the Approvals list, next to `group-queue-items` and `group-history-items` (unit tests). Status transitions reuse the existing service; no new transition.
- View state: parsing of the selected view, tab, range and display timezone from the URL, next to `parse-calendar-view-state` (unit tests), and the old-route mapping.
- Entry points under `app/(app)/publish/`: only call services (hard rule 3).
- UI: Playwright E2E golden path on `local` (ADR-149): switch views, open a post preview, expand, play a video, move a post from Approvals to Ready, empty Queue with suggestions; light, dark, 768 px and 390 px observed in the browser.
- Verified (staging) once per wave (ADR-153, ADR-161).

## Out of scope

- A layered approval workflow (approvers, enforcement, notifications for approval).
- Named saved views ("Save current view").
- User-set posting slots per channel.
- Group by anything other than Status; a separate Ready to Schedule column.
- Notes on posts ("Add note").
- New Outstand capabilities, best-time data from Outstand, or new analytics ingestion; suggestions use stored metrics only.
- Changes to the Draft Editor (ADR-155) beyond opening it with a preset date, time or account.
